02 Experiments

The lab bench

Interactive things I built from scratch in vanilla JavaScript, mostly to understand an idea by making it move. Everything below runs locally in your browser — no servers, no accounts, no tracking.

EXP-01 Grid Race
Flagship Neuroevolution Vanilla JS

No pre-trained model; just a fleet of F1 cars, each steered by a tiny neural network that starts as random numbers. They crash instantly at first. Every generation, the survivors that got furthest are bred together with a dash of mutation, and the field slowly learns the circuit. A different livery for every car. Watch them get faster, generation by generation.

Neuroevolution

26 neural nets, zero training data. Hit play and watch them learn to drive the circuit from scratch.
