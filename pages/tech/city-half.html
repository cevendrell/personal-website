    08 · Why this page exists

    What data and AI could do for Aarhus Motion

    I built this for my application to continue as an Aarhus Motion ambassador, using only the public results, OpenStreetMap and a GPS track of the course. Some things it could become:

    • A recap page after every race, with a shareable card for each runner.
    • A planning view for the organisers: try start waves, route changes or new aid stations against last year's field before race day.
    • Social clips: short flyovers of the course and the field for Instagram and the newsletter.
    • Community stories: which clubs, schools and companies grow, and who comes back from the winter runs to the City Half.

    Use the slider at the bottom to scrub through the morning, and drag the map to look around.
