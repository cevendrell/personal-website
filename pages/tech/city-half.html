    Two more years?

    I've been an Aarhus Motion ambassador for two years and would like to continue. I made this page with AI tools from the public results, OpenStreetMap and my GPS track of the race. Next term, I'd like to try:

    • Race cards after every event, like the one above, for runners to share.
    • Sub-2 and sub-1:30 social runs in the spring, for the hundreds who finish just around those times.
    • Team stories about the efterskoler, clubs and companies that bring big groups.
    • A replay like this one for the organisers, to try changes to the start or the course before race day.
